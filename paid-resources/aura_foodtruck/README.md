# aura_foodtruck

**aura_foodtruck** turns any vehicle into a fully functional food truck business. Employees cook menu items from real ingredients, serve walk-up NPC customers, ring up player orders at the register, and manage the entire operation from an in-game business tablet with upgrades, banking and activity logs.

Ships with **8 pre-configured branded trucks** — Snr Buns, Wig Wam, Bean Machine, Burger Shot, Cluckin' Bell, Greasy Joe's, Pizza This and Taco Farmer, each with its own recipes and UI theme.
